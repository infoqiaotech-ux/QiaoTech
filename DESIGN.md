## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an architectural bridge between imperial luxury and hyper-advanced artificial intelligence. Built for enterprise decision-makers, high-stakes infrastructure, and elite technology platforms, the aesthetic rejects clinical minimalism in favor of an evocative, high-prestige sensory environment. 

The emotional tone balances sovereignty, calculated precision, and visionary computation. Surfaces do not mimic standard software; they evoke illuminated physical conduits, dark obsidian glass, and electroplated antique metallurgy. The experience must feel ceremonial yet intensely responsive—merging deep celestial midnight depths with tactile golden engineering and cold cyan signals.

## Colors

The palette operates on calibrated luminescence within an absolute dark spectrum:

- **Base Void (`#0A1128`, `#0D1B2A`)**: The deep foundation. Dark celestial midnight, absorbing light to create zero-gravity depth across viewport canvases.
- **Surface Translucency (`#0D1B2A99`, `#132238CC`)**: Structured obsidian layers engineered with frosted backdrop diffusion.
- **Primary Imperial Gold (`#C9A25D`, `#D4AF7A`, `#E5C388`)**: Signifies value, structural integrity, high-tier status, and definitive action points. Never over-saturated; balanced like machined jewelry.
- **Secondary Cybernetic Cyan (`#3DDCFF`, `#00B4D8`)**: Emits high-frequency telemetry, live operational states, neural network triggers, and active cursor feedback.
- **Typography & Structural Slates**:
  - Main text: Crisp bone off-white (`#F5F5F0`) for high-contrast legibility.
  - Secondary/Telemetry text: Silver slate and polar ice (`#94A3B8`, `#A0AEC0`).
- **Glow & Structural Tracing**: Micro-luminescent strokes utilizing `#C9A25D33` (20% gold) for baseline resting edges, and `#3DDCFF40` for active sensory transitions.

## Typography

Typography orchestrates an editorial dialogue between mechanical authority and architectural majesty:

- **Display & Headings (Sora)**: Geometric, unapologetic, and balanced. Wide tracking is forbidden on display titles; keep letter-spacing compact to preserve optical weight.
- **Body Architecture (Manrope)**: Delivers effortless readability against deep black and dark navy canvas textures without visual fatigue.
- **Labels, Telemetry, and Identifiers (Space Grotesk)**: All category pills, system flags, and status lines employ uppercase typesetting paired with expanded tracking (`0.08em` to `0.14em`) to reference precision avionics and aerospace instrumentation.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column grid utilizing an 8pt rhythmic base. Content aligns strictly to geometric axes, allowing cards and interactive surfaces to emulate monolithic hardware blocks.

- **Desktop (>= 1280px)**: 12 columns, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters, `3rem` (48px) horizontal padding. Maximum content width constraints set to `1440px`.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: 8 columns, `1.25rem` (20px) gutters, `2rem` (32px) screen margins. Complex data layouts reflow into paired modular cards.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, `1rem` (16px) gutters, `1.25rem` (20px) screen margins. Dense telemetric modules pivot to vertical stack feeds with persistent horizontal card carousels for metrics.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through glassmorphic stratification and chromatic edge refraction rather than blurred dropshadows.

1. **Level 0 (Canvas)**: Solid base tone (`#0A1128`) layered with low-opacity cybernetic circuit grids and subtle radial ambient plumes (`rgba(61, 220, 255, 0.04)` and `rgba(201, 162, 93, 0.03)`).
2. **Level 1 (Structural Shells & Panels)**: Translucent navy fill (`#0D1B2A99`) with a hardware-grade `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a precise 1px gold perimeter trace (`border: 1px solid rgba(201, 162, 93, 0.15)`).
3. **Level 2 (Active Modules & Overlays)**: Surface tone (`#132238CC`), blur radius intensified to `24px`. Edges receive an asymmetric top-lit highlight via gradient borders (`linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(201, 162, 93, 0.4) 0%, rgba(61, 220, 255, 0.2) 50%, rgba(201, 162, 93, 0.05) 100%)`).
4. **Level 3 (Floating Menus & Critical Dialogs)**: Deep midnight glass with a faint cyan aura: `box-shadow: 0 20px 50px rgba(10, 17, 40, 0.8), 0 0 30px rgba(61, 220, 255, 0.1)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a restrained, chamfer-like corner rounding (`0.25rem` to `0.5rem`). Circular and ultra-round forms are strictly avoided, as high-curvature geometries soften the technical rigor and weaponized luxury of the visual identity. 

Components rely on architectural angles, hairline gold joints, and disciplined rectangular containers that communicate computational resilience.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Imperial Sovereign)**: Solid metallic gradient from `#C9A25D` through `#E5C388` to `#D4AF7A`. Rich dark obsidian text (`#0A1128`), weight 600. Subtle micro-shadow: `0 0 20px rgba(201, 162, 93, 0.3)`. Hover triggers a crisp cyan outer edge stroke.
- **Secondary (Cyber Conduit)**: Transparent glass panel with 1px border (`#C9A25D55`). Text rendered in bone white (`#F5F5F0`). Hover injects an internal cyan sweep: `background: rgba(61, 220, 255, 0.08)`, border changes to `#3DDCFF88`.
- **Tertiary / Utility**: Ghost button, uppercase `label-mono`, text in ice blue (`#94A3B8`) with glowing gold hover state.

### Chips & Badges
- Encased within 1px metallic borders with corner bracket accents.
- Upper-case `label-mono` typography. Status tags use cyan dot indicators pulsing through a double-ring keyframe animation.

### Form Inputs & Fields
- Background: `#0D1B2A80` inset.
- Resting Border: 1px hairline `#C9A25D33`.
- Active/Focus State: Border snaps to vibrant `#3DDCFF`, supported by an internal glow `box-shadow: inset 0 0 12px rgba(61, 220, 255, 0.15)`.
- Placeholder text in `#94A3B866`. Labels float above using `label-md` in `#C9A25D`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Sharp, soft-beveled squares (`roundedness: 1`).
- Inactive: Deep navy fill, gold-dusted stroke.
- Active: Filled with solid `#C9A25D`, checked using a razor-sharp inverted dark `#0A1128` glyph.

### Cards & Modules
- Outer container framed by semi-translucent midnight glass (`#0D1B2A99`) with backdrop blur.
- Cards feature micro-notches or hairline gold corner marks representing technological schematics. Header areas house high-contrast `label-mono` identifiers alongside electric cyan telemetry chips.

### Telemetry Nodes & Data Visualizations
- Charts use hyper-fine glowing paths (1.5px stroke width) in `#3DDCFF` with drop-shadow blooms.
- Data anchors are rendered as dual concentric circles: center gold core (`#C9A25D`), outer cyan radar ripple (`#3DDCFF44`).
